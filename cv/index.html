Research Focus

Understanding how neural networks represent and compute, and where these mechanisms come from. I focus on four questions:

  • Representations: what concepts intermediate representations encode, and what later layers read from them.
  • Computation: how features in superposition interact to implement computation.
  • Origins: how training, initialization, architecture, and data shape representational geometry, and how to control it.
  • Editing: changing a model’s knowledge and behavior directly in weight space.

Research Experience

Learning Quasar Variability with Generative Models [1]

2026
  • Trained a conditional flow matching model to learn the noise in quasar light curves, giving a realistic null model for detecting weak periodic signals.
  • Found that the model also learned rare signals hidden in the training data as part of the noise. Used the PCA spectrum of the latent embeddings to locate the low-variance directions holding them, and projected these directions out so the signals could be detected.

Guiding AlphaFold with Experimental Data [2, 6, 7]

2024 – 2026
  • Used pretrained protein structure models as a prior and steered their outputs to agree with experimental measurements.
  • Since many measurements are averages over many structures, built a guidance method that generates groups of related structures together so that they jointly match the data.
  • Implemented the forward model that turns a protein structure into an X-ray crystallography electron density map.

Embedding Geometry of Molecular Foundation Models [3]

2025
  • Analyzed the embedding space of foundation models trained on quantum-chemistry data, and found that it naturally clusters by biological and chemical properties the models were never trained on, such as secondary structure and chemical shifts.
  • Built zero-shot predictors from this geometry, and compared embeddings from different layers to see which transfer best.

Genetic Search on a Diffusion Model’s Manifold [5]

2025
  • Built a residue-wise latent mutation operator designed to keep samples near the generative model’s learned manifold, which makes genetic search possible when the objective is not differentiable.
  • Used it to search for protein structures that match experimental measurements.

Correcting Errors in Flow Matching Models [4]

2024
  • Studied the error that comes from the learned model itself, rather than from sampling with a finite number of steps.
  • Developed a cycle-consistent correction applied during sampling that pushes generated samples back toward the data distribution, improving sample quality on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet-64.

MRI Acquisition Guided by Importance Maps

2021
  • Designed MRI scanning trajectories in k-space that respect the scanner’s hardware limits while focusing on the most informative regions.
